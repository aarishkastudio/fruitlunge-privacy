Privacy Policy

Fruit Lunge · by Aarishka Studio · Last updated: October 17, 2026

In short: Fruit Lunge does not collect your personal information. Your game progress stays on your device. The game shows ads from Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising ID as described below.

This policy explains how the Fruit Lunge game ("the game", "we", "us") handles information. Fruit Lunge is published by Aarishka Studio.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or any other personal details. We do not run our own servers that receive your game data.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress on your own device so it is there the next time you play: your best score, coins and gems, the buckets, avatars and pets you have unlocked and equipped, your last cleared stage (checkpoint). This information never leaves your device and we cannot see it. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows banner ads provided by Google AdMob, a third-party advertising service. To show and measure ads, AdMob and its partners may collect and use certain information from your device, such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and operating system information, app and ad interactions, and approximate location derived from your IP address. This may be used to show ads that suit your interests (personalized ads) and to prevent fraud and measure ad performance. We do not receive your advertising ID or this information ourselves.

Learn more about how Google uses data: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Your choices. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads). In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and other regions where required, the game asks for your consent before showing personalized ads, and you can change your choice later.

Fonts

The game may load its text fonts from Google Fonts when your device is online. Requesting a font file can send your device's IP address to Google, as with any website or app that loads content from the internet. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Children's privacy

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help.

Sharing and selling information

We do not sell or share your personal information. Ads in the game are provided by Google AdMob, which handles the information described in the Advertising section under its own privacy policy.

Security and retention

Because we do not collect your data, we do not keep it. The data on your device stays there until you remove it by uninstalling the game or clearing its data.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the date above.
